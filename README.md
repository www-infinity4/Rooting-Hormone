# Rooting Hormone

A natural, banana-based rooting hormone solution for propagating plant cuttings. This project explores the science behind using banana as an effective, organic alternative to synthetic rooting compounds.

## About

Banana contains naturally occurring potassium, simple sugars, and auxin-like compounds that promote root development in plant cuttings. This project documents the science, method, and results of using banana-based rooting hormone.

## How It Works

### Potassium
Potassium regulates osmotic pressure inside plant cells, helping draw water and minerals into a cutting while it establishes new roots.

### Natural Sugars
Glucose and fructose in banana provide readily available energy for cellular growth at the base of the cutting.

### Moisture Retention
The banana flesh creates a moist environment around the cut end, reducing desiccation and promoting callus formation.

### Ethylene
As banana tissue breaks down it releases small amounts of ethylene, a plant hormone that stimulates rooting in many species.

## Usage

1. Take a healthy cutting (5–15 cm) just below a node.
2. Remove lower leaves, leaving two or three at the tip.
3. Push the cut end 2–3 cm into a fresh banana slice or coat it with mashed banana.
4. Plant the cutting in moist, well-draining propagation mix.
5. Keep in a warm, bright spot out of direct sun.
6. Expect roots in 2–4 weeks depending on the species.

## Tips

- Use a fresh banana slice or peel rather than a whole fruit to prevent excess rot.
- Change the banana material every 3–4 days if visible mold appears.
- Maintain soil moisture but avoid waterlogging.
